Why SConnect?

Sports communities are fragmented. Players struggle to find trials, coaches can't discover talent, clubs lack recruitment tools, and organizers juggle disconnected systems.

SConnect unifies the sports ecosystem with role-specific dashboards, secure authentication, and a discovery engine built for Indian sports — starting with Andhra Pradesh and Kerala.

  • Secure by default: Server-side sessions, Argon2id password hashing, role-based authorization
  • Privacy first: No tracking, no third-party analytics, your data stays yours
  • Location-aware: State → District → City → Village cascade for precise discovery
  • Open foundation: FastAPI + SQLite locally, PostgreSQL in production
